Privacy policy

Lure for Android · Last updated 9 October 2026

Lure is made by Luke Kavenagh ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game handles and where it goes. In short: the game itself collects no personal information, has no ads and no analytics, and never sends your data to me. The only outside services it uses are Google Play Games and Google Play billing, both run by Google.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, unlocked skills, settings, stats and run history are saved in a file on your phone. That file is never uploaded anywhere, and it is excluded from Android device backups. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Google Play Games

Lure uses Google Play Games for sign-in, achievements and the global leaderboard. When you are signed in, the game sends Google your achievement progress and your run scores (the XP earned in a run). Your scores appear on the leaderboard alongside your Play Games profile name and avatar, visible to other players according to your Play Games settings.

The game asks Google only for basic Play Games access. It does not request your email address, your contacts or any other Google account data. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can view, manage or delete your Play Games profile and data in your Google Play Games settings.

Purchases

The full-game unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and I never receive your card or payment details. The game asks Google Play whether you own the unlock so it can turn it on, including after a reinstall.

Like every developer, I receive the standard order records Google Play provides through the Play Console (such as order number, date, product, price and country), which I use for accounting and to handle refunds. I also receive an automatic alert when an unlock is purchased; it contains only the product name, nothing about who bought it.

Crash reports

If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may give me aggregated crash and performance statistics (Android vitals). These help me find and fix bugs, and do not identify you.

What Lure does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • I do not sell or share your information with anyone.

Children

Lure does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only personal data involved is what Google Play and Google Play Games handle under Google's own policies.

Changes

If the game starts handling information differently, I will update this page and the date at the top.
