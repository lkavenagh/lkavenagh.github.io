The game

You are a glowing particle, and everywhere you go you leave a trail. Enemies don't chase you, they chase your trail. Drag them into long, tangled lines behind you, then ignite: you race back along your own path and burn through everything that followed it.

Each zone brings a new kind of enemy that breaks the tactics the last one taught you. Between runs, the XP you earn unlocks a skill tree that changes how you hunt, how you survive and how your trail behaves.

Price

The first two zones are free. One purchase unlocks the rest of the game for good. No ads, no subscriptions, nothing else to buy.
